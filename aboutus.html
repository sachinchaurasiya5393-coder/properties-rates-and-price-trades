About Us

Who we are and what we do.

Who we are

T.Home is a focused house rental and property services platform for Indian cities. We help property owners list and manage properties while helping renters find verified homes quickly and securely. Our team combines local market expertise with modern tools to make renting and leasing simple.

What we offer

  • • Curated property listings across major Indian cities.
  • • Tenant screening and verification to reduce risk.
  • • End-to-end property management and maintenance coordination.
  • • Secure payment handling and transparent invoicing.
  • • Legal and lease support tailored for local regulations.

Benefits for owners & renters

For property owners

  • • Faster tenant placement and higher occupancy.
  • • Professional management and lower operational burden.
  • • Transparent fees and easy payout processing.

For renters

  • • Verified listings and honest property details.
  • • Simple booking and secure payment options.
  • • Support for leases, repairs and move-in coordination.
